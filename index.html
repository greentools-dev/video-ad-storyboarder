Video Storyboard Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

What is On-Screen Text (OST) and why is it essential?

Over 65% of mobile users browse TikTok and Instagram with sound muted. Having bold, punchy on-screen text matching the spoken script ensures complete retention even in silent feed environments.

How does the 30-second formula convert?

The structure begins with a 0-3 second visual pattern-interrupt hook, followed by 7 seconds of relatable pain agitation, 10 seconds of clear product demonstration, and ends with 10 seconds of social proof and direct call-to-action.
